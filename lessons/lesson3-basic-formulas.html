🧮 What is a Formula?

A formula is an instruction you give to the spreadsheet. It tells the spreadsheet what maths to do.

Every formula starts with an equals sign (=). If you forget the equals sign, the spreadsheet will think you are just typing words.

Example

If you type =2+2 and press Enter, the spreadsheet will show 4.

✏️ Step 1: Learn the Math Symbols

Spreadsheets use simple symbols for maths.

Symbol What it means Example
+Add=5+3
-Subtract=10-4
*Multiply=6*7
/Divide=20/4

🧪 Try It: Your First Formula

Click an empty cell.
Type =2+2.
Press Enter.
The answer should appear in the cell.

📦 Step 2: Multiply Price by Quantity

Imagine your table has a price in column B and a quantity in column C. You can multiply them to get a total in column D.

Click cell D2.
Type =B2*C2.
Press Enter.
The spreadsheet will multiply the number in B2 by the number in C2.
Important

The * symbol means multiply.

➕ Step 3: Use SUM

SUM adds a whole group of numbers for you.

Click the empty cell under your totals.
Type =SUM(D2:D5).
Press Enter.
The spreadsheet adds all the numbers from D2 to D5.
What D2:D5 means

This is a range. It means every cell from D2 down to D5.

📊 Step 4: Use AVERAGE, MAX, and MIN

These formulas help you understand your numbers quickly.

Formula What it does Example
AVERAGEFinds the middle value=AVERAGE(D2:D5)
MAXFinds the biggest number=MAX(D2:D5)
MINFinds the smallest number=MIN(D2:D5)
