🎮 Project 1: Ultimate Video Game Tracker

Create a spreadsheet to track your favourite video games.

Suggested headings

Game Title Platform Genre Hours Played My Rating (1-10) Completed?
MinecraftPCSandbox20010No
Mario Kart 8SwitchRacing509Yes
FortnitePCBattle Royale1208No
Zelda: BOTWSwitchAdventure8510Yes
RobloxPCSandbox3007No
Type the headings in Row 1.
Add at least 5 to 10 games.
Below the table, add formulas for total hours, average rating, highest rating, and total games.
Make a Bar Chart using Game Title and Hours Played.
Title the chart Time Spent on Each Game.
Helpful formulas

=SUM(...), =AVERAGE(...), =MAX(...), and =COUNT(...) are great for this project.

⚽ Project 2: Sports Season Tracker

Track your favourite sports team's season.

Game # Opponent Goals For Goals Against Result Points
1Tigers31Win3

4Lions40Win3
5Wolves13Loss0
6Bears31Win3
7Hawks22Draw1

Type the table into your spreadsheet.
Add a season summary underneath with totals for goals scored, goals conceded, points, wins, losses, and draws.
Use =COUNTIF(...) to count wins, losses, and draws.
Make a Pie Chart for wins, losses, and draws.
Make a Column Chart for goals for and goals against.
New formula idea

=COUNTIF(range,"Win") counts how many cells contain the word Win.

🎬 Project 3: Movie Night Planner

Create a spreadsheet to rate movies and find your favourites.

Movie Title Year Genre My Rating (1-10) Friend's Rating Watch Again?
Toy Story1995Animation98Yes
Frozen2013Musical89Yes
Shrek2001Comedy1010Yes
Minions2015Comedy76No
Moana2016Adventure99Yes
Type the headings and movie data.
Add averages for your ratings and your friend's ratings.
Use =MAX(...) to find the highest rating.
Use =COUNTIF(...,"Yes") to count how many movies you would watch again.
Colour rows green for Yes and red for No if you want a challenge.
