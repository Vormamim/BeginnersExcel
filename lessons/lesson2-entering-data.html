Learn how to type information into cells, edit it, and make your spreadsheet easier to read with simple formatting.

✏️ Typing Data Into Cells

Entering data in a spreadsheet is simple. Click a cell, type your information, and then press Enter to confirm it.

Click on a cell to select it.
Start typing. The content appears in the cell and in the formula bar.
Press Enter to move down or Tab to move right.
Type of data Example Notes
TextHello, PokemonUsually aligns left by default
Numbers42, 3.14Usually aligns right by default
Dates3/10/2025Spreadsheet apps often recognize date formats automatically

🛠️ Editing and Deleting Data

You can change or remove data after typing it.

Double-click a cell to edit it directly.
Or click the cell and press F2 to edit it.
To remove content, click the cell and press the Delete key.
To undo a mistake, press Ctrl + Z on Windows or Cmd + Z on Mac.
Practice idea

Change one value in your practice table, then undo it so you can see how editing and undo work together.

🔲 Selecting Multiple Cells

Sometimes you want to work with more than one cell at a time.

What to select How to do it
A single cellClick it
A range of cellsClick the first cell, hold Shift, then click the last cell
A whole rowClick the row number
A whole columnClick the column letter
All cellsPress Ctrl + A

A selected range is written like A1:B5.

🎨 Simple Formatting

Formatting makes your spreadsheet easier to read and more fun to use.

Tool What it does
BoldMakes important text stand out
ItalicChanges the style of the text
UnderlineAdds a line under the text
Fill ColorChanges the background color of a cell
Font ColorChanges the color of the text
AlignmentMoves text left, center, or right
Select the cells you want to format.
Use the toolbar or Home tab to apply bold, color, or alignment.
Try making the header row bold and giving it a background color.

↔️ Column Width and Alignment

Sometimes text is too wide for a cell, or you want it to sit neatly in the center.

To auto-fit a column, double-click the border between two column letters.
To change width manually, drag the border between columns.
To align text, select cells and choose left, center, or right alignment.
Watch for cut-off data

If text looks cut off or numbers do not fit, the column may need to be widened.
