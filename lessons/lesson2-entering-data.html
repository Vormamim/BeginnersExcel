Lesson 2: Entering Data

Learn how to type into cells, fix mistakes, and make your spreadsheet look neat and easy to read.

Start Lesson

🎯 Learning Goals

Type words and numbers into the correct cells
Move around the spreadsheet using Enter and Tab
Fix mistakes by editing or deleting cell contents
Make a simple table look neat with bold text, color, and alignment

✏️ Step 1: Type Into Cells

A spreadsheet is made of little boxes called cells. To put information into a cell, you click it and type.

Click once on cell A1.
Type Name.
Press Tab. This moves you one cell to the right.
Type Age.
Press Tab again.
Type Favourite Subject.
Press Tab again.
Type Score.
Press Enter. This moves you down to the next row.
What Enter and Tab do

Tab moves across. Enter moves down. These are two of the most useful keys in a spreadsheet.

Type of data Example What it means
TextAlex, Science, FootballWords
Numbers11, 8, 42Amounts you can count or calculate
Dates3/10/2025A calendar date

🛠️ Step 2: Fix Mistakes

Everyone makes mistakes while typing. Spreadsheets make them easy to fix.

Click the cell you want to change.
Double-click it if you want to edit the words or number already inside.
Press the Delete key if you want to clear the cell.
Press Ctrl + Z if you make a mistake and want to undo it.
Be careful

If you click a cell and start typing straight away, the old content will be replaced.

🧪 Try It: Change One Value

Click the score cell in row 2.
Double-click the cell.
Change the number to something different.
Press Enter.
Now press Ctrl + Z to undo the change.

🔲 Step 3: Select More Than One Cell

Sometimes you want to work with a whole group of cells at once.

What you want to select What to do
One cellClick it once
A group of cellsClick the first cell, hold Shift, then click the last cell
A whole rowClick the row number on the left side
A whole columnClick the letter at the top
EverythingPress Ctrl + A

If someone says a range like A1:D5, they mean all the cells from A1 to D5.

🧪 Try It: Select Your Table

Click cell A1.
Hold Shift.
Click the last cell in your table.
You should now see all of your table selected.

🎨 Step 4: Make It Look Neat

Formatting means changing how the spreadsheet looks. It does not change the data itself.

Tool What it does
BoldMakes text darker and easier to notice
Fill ColorChanges the background color of a cell
Font ColorChanges the color of the words or numbers
CenterMoves the text to the middle of the cell
Click and drag across your header row, or select the header cells.
Click Bold.
Choose a background color using the fill color button.
Click the Center button if you want the headings in the middle.

↔️ Step 5: Make Columns Wider

If words do not fit, you can make the column wider.

Move your mouse to the line between two column letters at the top, such as between A and B.
When the mouse pointer changes shape, double-click.
The column will grow to fit the longest word or number.
This is called auto-fit

Auto-fit is a quick way to stop words from being cut off.

💾 Step 6: Save Your Work

Click File.
Click Save As.
Choose a folder you will remember.
Name your file lesson2-entering-data.xlsx or something similar.
Click Save.
